About no.88

Spa quality, home comfort.

no.88 began as a simple idea: that proper, therapeutic treatments don't need a clinical spa to feel special. In a quiet, private room in Weybridge, every session is built around you — the pressure, the pace, the products — rather than a fixed script.

All treatments use natural, cruelty-free and vegan-friendly products from Neal's Yard and the Natural Spa Factory, chosen because they're genuinely good for your skin and for the environment.
